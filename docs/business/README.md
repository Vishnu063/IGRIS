# Business mission

`igris.business.BusinessController` tracks legitimate opportunity research and
planning independently from technical missions. Focuses cover DevOps, AWS,
websites, AI and business automation, maintenance, and digital products.
Technical IDs are references, never delegated permissions. Operator-supplied
objectives are sanitized. Records are in memory; no client was discovered,
contacted, acquired, charged or paid by creating a record.

Business planning is not authorization to incur AWS/API costs, activate an agent,
send a proposal or deploy. Those remain separately constrained. Revenue is never
guaranteed. Customer data must be minimized and explicitly approved for storage.
