# Synthetic opportunity evaluation

This is invented test data, not a job listing, customer inquiry or observed demand.
A hypothetical monitoring-maintenance request takes an estimated six hours;
proposed revenue is INR 3,000, expenses INR 0, competition 3/5 and feasibility 4/5.
The risks include no customer commitment and unverified monitoring access.
Changing expenses to INR 4,000 yields an estimated INR 1,000 loss.

The evaluator records requirements, effort, risks, competition, feasibility and
INR estimates in integer paise. Evidence retains a source and sample/operator
origin. Neither origin proves demand. Without evidence the outcome explicitly
requires market research. No job feeds, browsing automation or customers are
connected. Human review of dated source evidence is required before pursuing an
actual opportunity; quoted revenue must not become a confirmed payment.
